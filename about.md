I'm Arunraj M, an infrastructure engineer with 10+ years running production systems — AWS and Azure, Terraform/OpenTofu, Ansible, Docker, NGINX, Jenkins and GitHub Actions.

Most of my career has been inside private repositories, so this site and my [GitHub profile](https://github.com/arunrajm91) exist to make the work visible: Terraform modules and an Ansible role with real CI, an observability stack that encodes how I actually run management planes, and write-ups of the decisions behind migrations, rollbacks, and cost work.

What I care about, in practice:

- **Making trade-offs explicit.** A NAT-per-AZ decision or a private-cluster toggle belongs in a variable with a documented rationale, not buried in a diff.
- **Blast radius bounded by the platform.** An automation that *cannot* touch untagged resources beats one that merely *shouldn't*.
- **Rollback as a first-class requirement.** The measure of a migration plan is the cost of being wrong.
